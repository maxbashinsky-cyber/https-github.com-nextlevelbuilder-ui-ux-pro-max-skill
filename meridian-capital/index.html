Philosophy

Six principles. Zero exceptions.

Every position, every risk limit and every figure on this page flows from the same codified investment process — applied without discretion, published without edits.

Systematic Discipline

Every position is generated, sized and exited by code. No discretionary overrides — the models that produced fifteen years of simulated history run the live book unchanged.

Uncorrelated Engines

Four independent return streams — arbitrage, trend, mean reversion and volatility premia — blended so that portfolio correlation to equities sits near zero (beta 0.07).

Risk Before Return

A 10% portfolio volatility target, sleeve caps of 10–40%, and an automatic kill-switch that de-risks any sleeve breaching 2× its historical drawdown. Risk is budgeted first; return follows.

Radical Transparency

Every figure on this page is reproducible from our research engine — and the full analytics suite is public. Inspect the Monte Carlo, the drawdowns and the correlations yourself.

Alignment

The principals invest pari passu alongside every limited partner, and performance fees accrue only above a high-water mark. We eat our own cooking — same share class, same terms.

Institutional Infrastructure

Independent fund administrator, annual third-party audit and segregated custody. Counterparty, valuation and operational risk are handled to allocator due-diligence standards.

The Engine

Four sleeves. One uncorrelated machine.

Each sleeve targets a distinct, persistent source of return. Risk-parity weighting blends them into the Flagship — engineered for the smoothest possible path.
